NOON TRACKS · OBJECTIVES + ANNOUNCEMENT · SUBJECT-PROOF DIRECTIONS · WORST-CASE SUBJECTS SHOWN

Moment pages that no subject colour can break

Problem: Objectives (sage band) and Announcement (saffron band) collide when the subject itself is sage or saffron. These directions move the moment identity onto something no subject owns — ink, iconography, or layout — leaving the subject colour free in the rail. Left mock = Objectives on a sage subject; right = Announcement on a saffron subject.

A · Ink header band

The band survives but turns INK — the guide’s “focus” colour, unownable by any subject. Moment icon + name live in the band in cream; subject rail continues below it. Shown in worst cases: sage subject behind Objectives, saffron subject behind Announcement — no clash.

B · Icon-led, no band

No band at all. The moment is announced by a large deep-tone line icon (target / megaphone) on the illustration side + a heavy ink title with grey underline block. Ink diamonds so numbering is moment-neutral. Lightest touch; moment pages become siblings of working pages.

C · Ink interruption pages

Objectives and Announcement become full ink pages — the deck “dims the lights” for a beat. Cream text and icon, subject colour reduced to a thin keyline + swipes. Most dramatic and utterly subject-proof; announcement especially benefits.

Recommendation: A (ink header band). It keeps everything you liked about the band system — instant recognition, icon + name — but ink can never collide, and the guide already assigns ink to “focus” moments. C is a strong second if you want these beats to feel bigger; B is the quietest but loses the “moment interrupt” feeling.
