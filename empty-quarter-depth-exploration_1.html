Flat core, living edge

Calibrated to the countdown numeral: flat is still the main direction. No screen below uses lighting for depth — no gradient washes, no shading, no cast shadows. Depth comes from three flat-native moves: edge quality (rough, turbulence-chewed silhouettes with grit at the rim), opacity steps (fainter hairline = further away), and scatter (loose grains around bodies, occluded by whatever passes in front). The "gold" is never a new colour — it is the existing cream→sand→clay ramp, one shimmer seam per screen. Type stays ink-crisp on top. Motion is garnish: everything must read at a standstill.
