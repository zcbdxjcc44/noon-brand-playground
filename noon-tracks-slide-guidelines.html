Slide template guidelines

The system in one sentence: bright family-core covers and ceremonial moments, calm plaster working pages, one saffron accent voice, ink for reading. Everything below serves that sentence. Where this document and the Empty Quarter v4 guide disagree, the EQ guide wins on colour meaning; this document wins on slide mechanics.

03 · Type scale

Arabic: Vazirmatn. Latin: Hanken Grotesk. Mono meta: JetBrains Mono. Six steps, no in-betweens — nothing on a content page goes below 40:

TOKENSIZE / WEIGHTUSED FOR
Hero XL130 Bold · lh 162 (ar) / 150 (en)Cover title, Section/Game hero
Hero L104 Bold · lh 128Announcement headline, Ending hero
Hero M76 BoldQuote, Objectives headline
Title64 BoldWorking-page titles
Prompt48 Medium · lh 72Prompt/question text
Body & meta40 · weight by roleEverything else: body (Reg), list items (Med), numerals (Bold), captions, kickers, durations — the floor
Rules: if a new text doesn't fit a token, pick the nearest token — don't mint a new size. A title wrapping past 3 lines drops one token. Covers are exempt from the 40 floor: cover chips (24) and descriptors (32) keep their sizes; the noon Tracks lockup is a fixed brand mark, not type.

05 · Component grammar

Chip — subject identity on covers only (y=151). Auto-layout family-light pill, family-deep text. Content pages carry the subject through the rail and accent colour instead — no chip, more room for content.

Wave — the deck's signature underline. One per page under the title. Never two.

Diamond — 56px rotated square, r=5, family core, ink numeral (24 Bold). Sequence marker only; when the relationship is not sequential, drop numerals and use arrows.

Swipe — family-light highlighter bar behind a hero line's final words. Hug the text width (+40px), sit at ~55% of the line height. Ceremonial and moment pages only.

Arrows — hand-drawn curved strokes (ink 70%, 3.5px, round caps) with open arrowheads. They carry relationships between coloured boxes; redraw freely per lesson (branch, merge, loop).

Illustration — fine single-stroke linework only. On cores: cream mains (85–90%) + one family-deep accent cluster. Never filled clip-art, never more than one dark cluster.

06 · Don'ts

No pure white fills · no new font sizes (six steps, floor 40) · nothing smaller than 40px on content pages · no chips on content pages · no second wave per page · no sage/saffron moment bands (subjects own those hues — moment identity is ink) · no violet or orange decoration (AI and Heat own them) · covers never reuse the olive-dim deeps as grounds — deep is for linework and text only.

Ratio check (from EQ v4): if a working page feels loud, the ratio has slipped — plaster should still dominate ~60%, ink ~30%, family colour ~10%. The colour lives in the rail, chip, wave, diamonds, and one motif — nothing else.
