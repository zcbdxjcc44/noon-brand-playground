05

Pattern system

الأنماط

Three pattern languages: Facet, Khatam and Pinboard. Each is shown in both voices, at scale, and in UI. Facet is taken directly from the classroom walls: one faceted lattice in two intensities, dunes (orange) and plaster (light). Abstract, the desert as geometry, from a wall mural down to a 14px app texture.

Which pattern, when
Facet · the landscape
Hero surfaces and moments of arrival: murals, welcome headers, terrain, splash. Big and warm, never behind dense text.
Khatam · the ceremony
Certificates, milestones, signage and dividers. Always line, never fill, used sparingly: when it appears, it means something.
Pinboard · the map
Maps, facades and quiet texture behind surfaces. A clean even field; green marks only the highlighted pin.
Pattern misuse
Learn.
✕Don’t set text directly on the lattice — use a gradient or plain band.
✕Don’t fill khatam shapes solid — it is always a line pattern.
✕Don’t fade pinboard dots — they are full-strength or absent.
✕Don’t mix two pattern languages on one surface.

Construction
GRID 64 · LIVE AREA 48 · STROKE 2 · CAPS ROUND · JOINS ROUND
Every icon is drawn on a 64 grid with a 48 live area, one 2px stroke, round caps and joins. No fills, no second weight, no detail smaller than 2px. If it can’t survive these constraints, simplify the object.
One line, one weight
A single 2px stroke, no fills, geometry over realism. If it needs shading, it is too detailed.
Drawn like a plan
Objects are reduced to their construction — circles, facets and seams, the same discipline as the patterns.
Brand colours only
Terracotta on light grounds, cream on ink. Never blue, never green — colour roles belong to the UI, not the drawings.

Progression

التقدّم

Progress is water. Every passage cleared fills the jug, water is the currency of ground covered in the desert. It is always drawn in water blue and only water blue: the path travelled, the current position, a chosen answer. Waypoints are the checks along the route — a diamond fills when a checkpoint is cleared, a triangle marks where you stand. Green appears once, at arrival.

Water is the currency
Ground covered becomes water gathered. Fill levels, jugs and progress lines are always water blue, and only water blue.
Waypoints are the checks
A diamond is a checkpoint: filled once cleared, a triangle where you stand now, open ahead. Signals along the route, never scores.
Arrival is green
Green appears exactly once on a route — at arrival. One green moment keeps it worth walking towards.
