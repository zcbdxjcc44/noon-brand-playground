The visual language of the Noon Academy design system. Spatial rather than decorative: one focal point per screen, lines that each carry one meaning, and luminous white as the only signal. Fog by day, Void by night, green for action, gold for the journey.

02

Palette

اللون

Quiet neutrals carry every screen: Fog for daylight, Void for night, Ink and Plaster for type. Gold is the identity colour: it draws the journey. Luminous white is the signal for where you are now. Every other colour is a role with one job, and terracotta is a small heat accent, not the brand voice.

Proportion of use

If a screen feels loud, the ratio has slipped. Clay is the smallest slice on purpose.

55 · Fog / Void ground
25 · Ink / Plaster type
10 · Raised

About 90% of any screen is neutral. The last sliver is shared, in order of frequency, by green (action), gold (journey), the white signal (now, one per screen), water (selection), teal (analysis), purple (AI tutor) and clay (heat). No role colour is ever a full-screen field.

The signal · luminous white

Not a colour in the palette, an emphasis device. It means one thing: where you are now.

One per screenIf two things glow, neither is the focal point. Move the signal; never add a second one.

03

Typography

الخط

A light geometric face for display and numerals, a clean sans for the interface, mono for running numbers and a dot-matrix face for status. Precision comes from thin strokes and spacing, not from light. Emphasis comes from colour or weight, never from italic. Arabic is set at the same scale as Latin.

04

Lines & patterns

الخطوط والأنماط

Spatial, not decorative. Every line carries one meaning: the terrain is the quietest, the route shows direction, and only the signal glows or moves. The grounds carry information too: stars are the sky you reach for, the terrain field is the map you cross, the grid is the chart paper the route is plotted on.

The line system

Only the signal may glow or move, and there is at most one signal per screen.

Terrain

The quietest line. Map screens only, and it fades before it reaches content.

0.5px · chalk 4–8% · still

Route

Direction. Solid behind, dashed ahead, an arrowhead where it turns.

1.5px · gold · no glow

Now. The current waypoint, the speaker, the countdown, a milestone at the moment it is reached.

Dividers only. Wherever space can separate two things, it does, and no box is drawn.

01

Star field

النجوم

Chalk stars with a real magnitude spread: many faint, few bright. The brightest form small constellations joined by tapered lines, and the very brightest get a halo and diffraction spikes. Stars are ideas, the lines are the connections you make between them, and the groupings are never fixed, because every learner connects things differently.

02

Terrain field

التضاريس

A topographic map of difficulty. Contour lines crowd where the terrain is steep and spread where it is easy, so a student sees at a glance where the hard parts are. Every fifth contour is bolder: those are the milestones. The gold route traces the path of least resistance through the valleys, the proven route a tutor would recommend.

Top-down · tilt 0 · no route

03

Grid paper

ورق الرسم

A 64px grid in chalk at 4% on void or ink at 5% on paper, with every fourth line twice as strong. Corner ticks in gold mark a framed area, the way a surveyor marks a plot. It is the only pattern allowed under dense text.

Khatam

الخاتم

A square and a diamond interlocked, drawn in plaster hairline at 6% and never filled. It holds still behind the countdown and frames nothing else. The white ring in front of it is the signal and runs down with the seconds; the khatam is structure, so it does not move.

07

Progression

التقدّم

Progress is a route on a map. The line is gold and shows direction: solid behind the student, dashed ahead, an arrowhead where it turns. Where the student is now is the white signal, the one lit point on the route. The destination is a gold dot in a soft gold halo.

The route is gold

The line, the points already reached and the milestones earned. One route per view, and it never glows.

White is where you are

The current waypoint is a white ring: the largest mark on the route and the only one that is lit. On Fog it becomes a solid ink ring.

Water is what you chose

A selected answer, an active tab. It never draws progress, so the route keeps one colour.

Waypoints · two forms

Same states, same colours. The number is added only when it helps.

States never changeIn both forms: gold dot reached, white ring current, hairline ahead, a gold dot in a soft halo for the destination.
One form per routeNever mix numbered and unnumbered points on the same route.
The current one is always largestWhichever form, the white ring is the biggest and only lit point, so the eye lands there first.
Arrival is quietReaching a waypoint takes 420ms: the ring moves on and the point behind it shrinks to a gold dot. Nothing else on the screen moves.

08

Guiding Lights layer

الأضواء الهادية

An optional ceremonial layer on top of Proven Routes, for covers, countdowns and celebrations. It adds no new colours. It gives the north star a job, adds the astrolabe ring and turns rewards into coins.

AddsNorth star
Marks the destination at the end of a route. It never moves; the waypoints do.
AddsAstrolabe ring
Concentric rings and radials in gold at 14%. Ceremonial screens only.
AddsBadge coins
Subjects, teams and rewards as objects. Flat face, one hairline, no gradient.
AddsOne gold node per icon
Ceremonial icons get one filled gold point that marks what matters.
KeepsEvery colour, type face, spacing token and component from chapters 01–07.

Astrolabe ring behind a countdown · ceremonial only

Badge coins

Earned is the only gold coin. Every other coin wears its own subject or team colour.

Rules of the layer

G1Ceremonial screens only: covers, countdowns, celebrations, certificates. Working screens stay plain Proven Routes.
G2No route, no north star. An ambient star field needs nothing added.
G3One ground per view. The astrolabe ring never sits under contours.
G4No gradients, on coins or anywhere else. Metal is a flat face and one lighter hairline.
G5Decorative colour (gold plus subject tints) stays near 10% of a view.
G6On paper, the route and waypoints are drawn in ink. The earned coin is the only gold object on paper.
