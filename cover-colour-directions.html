Cover colour — three ways out of “serious”

The current covers all sit on deep, dimmed grounds — dramatic but sombre, and side by side they read as four shades of boardroom. Three palette-faithful ways to warm them, shown across all four subjects:

A · Core grounds — colour does the talking

Ground becomes the family CORE (saffron #DDBA62, plum #AC7F97, sage #9FAA7D, sky #86A7BE) with ink text and a soft cream illustration. Brightest, most high-school; still 100% palette. Content pages already speak this colour, so the deck clicks together.

B · Light grounds — warm pastel poster

Ground becomes the family LIGHT tint with ink text and the illustration drawn in the family deep. Softest and friendliest; risk: less drama, covers may feel like content pages.

C · Keep deep, add the golden hour

Deep grounds stay for drama, but the illustration switches to the bright family core (like the current Chemistry غلاف), plus a colour swipe behind the title. Least change; covers stay moody but gain warmth.

Recommendation: A. Core grounds are the honest fix — the covers finally look like the subject icons that inspired them, ink text keeps them readable, and the bright cover + calm plaster content pages give the deck a natural rhythm.
