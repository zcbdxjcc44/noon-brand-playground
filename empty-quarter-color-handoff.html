A colour-only diff between your original Empty Quarter palette (the ten locked colours) and the current V2.2 application of it. Short version: your palette is intact. FROM JI · TO THE AUTHOR OF THE EMPTY QUARTER PALETTE

Every colour you locked is kept at the same hex — with one exception: oak deep is desaturated a touch. Two things still need your call — flagged at the end. Nothing here overrides your definitions; treat it as a proposal on top of them.
