The Empty Quarter

الرُّبع الخالي — نظام الهوية البصرية

A Saudi-native visual system for the hybrid classroom. Heritage moves to future, left to right — earth, timber, intelligence. The rooms are warm matter; the screens are warm light. Every session is an expedition: routes, waypoints, water, terrain, crews. Pattern constructions in this document are generated by the canonical algorithms from empty-quarter-main; the color layer is the amended palette.

01

Color

اللون

Surfaces carry place; roles carry meaning. Screens do not copy the room's pigment — they harmonize under its light. The interior palette is reflective; the UI palette is emissive, so accents brighten on ink.

Timber is material, not ornament. Warm oak and oak deep exist for the physical world and for depicting wood — interiors collateral, signage, furniture, illustration, artwork ramps. Neither appears as a UI surface or accent; on ink, oak's warmth survives only as the type color. And oak deep is not a decorative gold — Saffron decorates, oak builds; using timber as ornament would resurrect the retired gold through the back door. Cream fields were the notebook; the fix was inversion, not saturation. The artwork ramps inside the patterns (dunes, plaster voices, the mural strata) are pattern-intrinsic and remain as drawn.

Decorative · ornament only · للزينة فقط

Five families for subject tags, avatar fills, and illustrative icons — desaturated, warm-gray undertone, three steps each (light · core · deep). Orange is deliberately absent: Heat owns that hue, and ornamental orange reads as a warning. Violet is absent too: the AI owns it. Plum is built pink-leaning, a quarter-wheel from the tutor's violet; Sky is lighter and airier than Water and is barred from selection or progression UI.

Ration it. Decorative color never carries state and never leads a screen. Functional icons stay monochrome — decorative color applies only to subject tags, avatar fills, and spot illustration, in small areas, one family per element and rarely more than two families in view. Assignments are stable: a subject keeps its family everywhere (Reading is always Sky). Steps by surface: core on fog, light on ink, deep only as text on its own tint. Semantic accents always outrank ornament. When in doubt, leave it cream.

04

Pattern system

الأنماط

Three pattern languages — Facet, Khatam, Pinboard — each with a construction, a set of voices, and a scope. Everything below is generated live by the canonical algorithms.

Facet — one lattice, three voices · كثبان

A jittered grid of planes: roughly half the cells split into triangle pairs, the rest remain quads. A low-frequency tone field clusters shades into larger masses, like the murals. The geometry is generated once; the voices are palettes mapped onto the identical planes.

Dunes — the orange voice, cream seams (rgba 241,235,221 · .35). Hero brand moments: session titles, the one hero card per ink screen, murals.

Plaster — the light voice, white seams (.5). Quiet faceted grounds that content sits on.

Banner — a quiet dune: the same lattice on a deliberately narrow tonal range (#B04F30 → #C6663F) so UI text stays legible on top. For announcement banners and dressed cards.

Applied · terrain, built from the lattice

Eight sinusoid dune ridges from cream to deep rust, strip-triangulated. Every ridge carries its own seam breakpoints, so no vertical line runs straight through; ridges are clamped never to cross. White seams at .4.

UI additions (amended): the quiet line voice — the same lattice rendered stroke-only in cream on ink, one pen with the Khatam — for ambient grounds on instrument screens; and the plateau rule — any terrain under a navigation zone settles to an unbroken plane (min ~72px + safe area, one seam at its top edge). Fingers land on calm ground.

Khatam · خاتم

The canonical form is a 96px repeating tile — square and diamond interlocking, stroke 1.1, ink at .22 on light (teal and cream variants exist). Line only, never filled, never dense.

Enlarged as a single ceremonial ornament: the countdown, certificates, milestones. It rotates glacially (150s / 210s counter) or not at all. Scope: signage, watermarks, dividers — sparing, always.

Pinboard · لوحة الدبابيس

The Kingdom, drawn in pins. No outline — the perforation itself is the territory; where the pins stop, the border is. Even 12.5 grid, terracotta at .38 on light (cream on ink). Green pins mark the highlight: a city, a live room, an active crew.

The facade dissolve — the one sanctioned thinning: around a clear zone the field drops pins by discrete parity rings, so it reads as perforation thinning out, not a punched hole. No opacity fades, ever.

Scope (amended): Pinboard lives in bounded zones — maps, attendance, facades, empty states — and as data (present / absent / highlighted pins). Never a full-screen wallpaper behind data displays.
05

The route

المسار

The waypoint grammar — inherited from Proven Routes, now the system's spine. Every journey is drawn the same way, from a two-row list to a full concept map.

Diamond = waypoint. Triangle = you. Solid line behind, dotted line ahead. Filled = done or present; hollow = open; hairline = locked; green = arrival. Fill is a state, never a style. Water marks the current position wherever the route grammar meets selection (tabs, chosen answers).
06

Iconography

الأيقونات

Icons join the drawing language instead of importing one. Line-only, 1.7 stroke on a 24px grid, miter joins, flat terminals. Angles snap to 0°/45°/90°; where a generic set rounds a corner, this one cuts it at 45°. Curves survive only where meaning demands them — heads, lenses, clock faces. Directional icons mirror in RTL. If an icon is missing, it is drawn to these rules — never dropped in from a third-party set.

07

Motion

الحركة

Restraint is the personality. Three working durations — 120 / 200 / 320ms — cover all UI transitions. Beyond them, only two registers exist:

  • Ceremony is glacial. The Khatam turns at 150s per revolution, its inner ring counter-rotating at 210s. At that speed it doesn't spin — it lives.
  • Ticks settle. A countdown digit arrives at scale .94 → 1 with a soft ease — weight landing, not bouncing.
  • Emergence is the transition. When a classroom takes the stage, nearby field pins drift into its ring (~600ms). The metaphor is performed, not stated.
  • One thing glows. The live element may breathe; nothing else moves at rest.
