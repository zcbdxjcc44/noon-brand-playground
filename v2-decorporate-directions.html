V2 feels corporate — three ways to warm it up

The diagnosis: dimming saffron to #6E5824 for readability turned it olive — a boardroom colour — and it only ever appears as slivers. High-school energy needs the bright half of the subject family doing visible work. Deep tones should be for reading, bright tones for feeling. Mocks shown RTL (rail right) to match the current deck; Arabic swaps in unchanged.

A · Let the gold out — bright core colour

The layout stays; the colour swaps. The rail, chip, wave, diamonds and motif switch from the murky deep #6E5824 to the icon’s real saffron gold. Deep tone only survives in text. Cheapest fix, biggest mood shift.

B · Tinted cards + chunky rail

Bright gold rail gets thicker; the chip goes solid; cards fill with family light-tints (saffron light for body, sage/rose lights on the 3-points) instead of clinical white. Warmest, most “classroom poster”. Diamonds pick up the family trio again.

C · Poster energy — same palette, bigger voice

Colours stay as they are today, but every character element grows: motif fragments 2–3× larger and darker, oversized diamonds, a gold highlighter swipe behind the title, chunkier waves and sparks. For when you want the current palette but louder.

Recommendation: A as the base, with B’s tinted cards on the 3-points/agenda rows only. A fixes the actual problem (the colour) without adding noise; B’s tints give list-type slides the warmth of the old deck; C alone risks “louder boardroom”. Covers keep their deep grounds — the contrast between a deep cover and golden content pages actually helps the deck breathe. Same recipe maps to every subject: Biology mauve → bright rose-mauve core, Future sage → bright leaf sage.
