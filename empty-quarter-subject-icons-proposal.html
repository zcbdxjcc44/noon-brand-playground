أيقونات المواد — الرُّبع الخالي

Five subjects — Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Math, English — drawn to the Empty Quarter iconography rules: line-only, 1.7 stroke on a 24px grid, miter joins, flat terminals, angles snapped to 0°/45°/90°, corners cut rather than rounded, curves kept only where meaning demands. Facet geometry

الأوجه — الاتجاه الجذري

The radical take. Every subject is rebuilt from the expedition geometry — waypoint diamonds, octagons, cut facets, zero incidental curves. The atom's orbits become nested rotated squares; the flask becomes a faceted vessel with a coloured liquid facet; biology is an angular sprout of diamond leaves; math sits inside the settings-octagon; English is a faceted capital with a filled counter. One family facet per icon carries the colour. This is the direction that looks unmistakably Empty Quarter.

Grid & rules used

الشبكة والقواعد
  • 24×24 grid, single 1.7 stroke, stroke-linecap:butt, stroke-linejoin:miter.
  • Angles snap to 0° / 45° / 90°; corners are cut at 45° rather than rounded (see the flask base, the octagon).
  • Curves survive only where meaning demands — orbits, leaf, book pages, flask liquid.
  • Directional glyphs would mirror in RTL; none here are directional.
  • Decorative family colour is one facet per icon, never carrying state; heat / water / AI hues untouched.
