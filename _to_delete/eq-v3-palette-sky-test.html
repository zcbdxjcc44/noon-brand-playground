EMPTY QUARTER · V3 PROPOSAL · TEST 01 · PALETTE + SKY ONLY

Does inverting figure and ground fix the seriousness?

One cover and one ceremonial slide, each shown as v4 today and as v3 proposed. Layout, type, illustration, band, seam and lockup are identical in every pair. The only changes are which colour holds the field, the addition of an atmospheric sky, and the demotion of saturated terracotta to a framing role. No new hexes are introduced — every value below comes from the colleague's locked ten or the approved cover material set.

01 · Tracks cover — Option D anatomy, two grounds

Same slim 132px ink band, same foil seam, same v10-grain planet stacked over the band, same title position and lockup. Left is today's stage ground #B65534. Right replaces it with a three-plane haze sky.

02 · Ceremonial slide — "turn the lights up", two ways

The Tracks spec sends ceremonial pages to a full-bleed family core. Left is that, in saffron #DDBA62. Right keeps the drama but produces it with light instead of saturation: luminous sky field, one monumental faceted form, saffron kept as the sun and the accent furniture.

03 · Measured colour share

Pixel-counted from the rendered stages, not estimated. Saturated warm = warm hue at saturation ≥ 30% and lightness ≤ 68%. Light warm = plaster, cream, sand. The target from the proposal was saturated terracotta at or below 12–15%; both v3 surfaces land inside it. Cool reads 0.0% and that is the honest result — water at 15% over a warm ground desaturates rather than shifting hue, so the atmospheric shadow shows up as the fall in saturated warm, not as blue pixels. If you want cool to be legible as cool, it has to go up to roughly 30% opacity or move to the deepest plane.
