NOON TRACKS · COVER SET · BUILT FROM THE SAFFRON CHEMISTRY COVER

One recipe, five subjects

Nothing new is invented here. The chemistry cover already in presenter-opening-empty-quarter.html is taken apart into five layers, and the same five layers are rebuilt for the other four families. Full-bleed family core stays — the cover never felt serious because of its colour, it would only feel serious if it were empty. Every motif is drawn the way the sphere-and-orbit is drawn: flat 2D shapes and thin lines, no modelling, no facet rocks.

01 · The five layers

L1

Ground

Family core, full bleed. No gradient, no vignette, no scenery.

L2

Ambient lattice

A large tiling in core lightened ~5%, stroke 1.5. Barely visible. Its geometry echoes the motif — hexes for chemistry, rings for quant, mesh for skills.

L3

The motif

Cream line at 55–70%, stroke 3, drifting diagonally in the outer third. One filled shape, a few dots. Never centred, never symmetrical.

L4

Punctuation

Two four-point sparks and one plus, scattered into the empty middle. This is the layer that stops the cover reading as corporate.

L5

Text block

Ink title right-aligned, swipe in family light behind the last line only, short deep rule, subject label, lockup. The swipe is authored, not computed — write titles so the final line is one or two short words, or it wraps and stripes twice.

The sphere-and-orbit rule, generalised. A motif may use flat value zones — a shape cut by a straight chord into light and deep — but never shading, never a gradient inside a zone, never a modelled surface. The depth comes from the line work sitting over the flat zones. Only the sky cover uses filled zones here; the other four are line and dots alone, which is the quieter end of the same grammar.
